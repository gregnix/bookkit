# Introduction

This example book shows the bookkit conventions on a minimal case. A key term is
marked as [version control]{.index} and thus enters the subject index.

How the actual work flows is covered in the [Fundamentals](#fundamentals) chapter
— the cross-reference uses the title anchor.

## Structure

Headings carry no numbers; the build adds them. Sections become sub-numbers
(1.1, 1.2 …) and appear in the table of contents.
